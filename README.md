# CorpseRun

A Minecraft 1.21.1 mod for **NeoForge** and **Fabric**.

When you die, you come back as a **Ghost** and your body stays behind where you last stood on solid ground. Run back to your corpse and resurrect.

Can't reach your body? `/resurrect` works anywhere: away from your corpse it asks you to confirm with `/resurrect accept` within 10 seconds, and you come back where you stand, without the protection a proper corpse run earns.

Works with [Sable](https://github.com/ryanhcode/sable) / Create Aeronautics: die on an airship and your corpse stays on it, wherever it flies.
